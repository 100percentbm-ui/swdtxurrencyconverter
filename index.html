How it works

Three ways in, five formats out.

Bring in a number Screenshot, paste, drag a file, capture your screen, or just type it
Unshorten reads it On-device OCR finds the digits and the K/M/B/T suffix — crop, rotate, or hand-tune threshold if it needs help
Copy in the format you need Plain, grouped, currency, scientific, or re-abbreviated shorthand
